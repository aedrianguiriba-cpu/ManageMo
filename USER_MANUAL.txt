1. ABOUT MANAGEMO
----------------------------------------------------------------------

ManageMo keeps track of the equipment owned by the university. It lets
you see what is available, ask to borrow or receive an item, ask for a
repair, and follow your request until the item reaches you.

There are two kinds of accounts:

  USER   Browses inventory, submits requests, and tracks them.
  ADMIN  Manages inventory, decides on requests, delivers items,
         retires old equipment, and manages accounts and settings.

Which pages you see depends on your account type. You cannot open the
other kind of page. If you try, you are sent back to your own
dashboard.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. GETTING STARTED (EVERYONE)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Create an account

  1. Open the ManageMo website. The login page appears.
  2. Choose Sign Up.
  3. Enter your full name, email, a password of at least 6 characters,
     the same password again, and your department (college, office or
     campus).
  4. Submit the form.
  5. A message asks you to log in. Your account is ready right away.
     There is no email to confirm.

  Every self-registered account is a USER account. Administrator
  accounts are created by the system owner, not on the sign-up page.

2.2 Log in

  1. Enter your email and password on the login page.
  2. Choose Log In.
  3. You land on your dashboard: the user dashboard for users, the
     admin dashboard for administrators.

  If you see "Invalid email or password", check both and try again.
  If the details are right, your account may have been deactivated.
  Contact an administrator.

2.3 Forgot your password

  1. On the login page, choose Forgot Password.
  2. Enter your email.
  3. Open the email and click the reset link.
  4. Type a new password and save it.
  5. Log in with the new password.

2.4 The notification bell

  The bell at the top of every page shows messages about your
  requests, for example when a request is approved or an item is out
  for delivery.

  - Click a notification to open the page it refers to.
  - Choose Mark all read to clear the list.

2.5 Log out

  Open the account menu at the top right and choose Logout. Always log
  out on a shared computer.

3. USER GUIDE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Dashboard

  The first page after login. It shows:
  - Summary cards for your requests and borrowed items.
  - A calendar of return dates.
  - Quick actions for common tasks.
  - A short list of recent requests and the items you own.
  - A browse-and-reserve list of available items.

3.2 Inventory

  Menu: Inventory

  A read-only list of what the university has. You cannot change
  anything here.

  - Available tab: items you can ask for.
  - Borrowed tab: items that are currently lent out.
  - Department Owned Items tab: items owned by your department.
  - Use the search box, category filter and owner filter to find
    something.
  - Choose Reserve on an available item to start a request for it.

3.3 Submitting a request

  Menu: Requests

  Choose the type of request:

  BORROW   Use a catalog item for a limited time and return it.
  ITEM     Ask for an item that you will keep (procurement). Pick a
           catalog item, or type the name of an item that is not in
           the catalog.
  SERVICE  Ask for maintenance or repair. Describe the problem.

  Steps:

  1. Choose the request type.
  2. For Borrow and Item, pick the item from the grid. For Borrow, the
     availability calendar shows when the item is free. Set the date
     you will return it.
  3. For Service, describe what is wrong and where.
  4. Set the urgency: low, medium, high or critical.
  5. Choose how you want to receive it: delivery or pickup.
  6. Give the reason for the request.
  7. Submit.

  If you ask for several units, each unit becomes its own request, but
  they stay together as one group so you can follow them as one.
  Administrators are notified when you submit.

  Tip: Terms may read differently on your site. An administrator can
  switch the wording so that Borrow reads "Transferrable" and Request
  reads "Procurement".

3.4 Following your requests

  Menu: My Requests

  Each request group is a card with a progress bar:

    Submitted -> Under Review -> Approved or Disapproved
              -> Out for Delivery -> Delivered

  - If a request is disapproved, the reason is shown on the card.
  - When an item is out for delivery you receive an email and a
    notification.

3.5 Your records

  Menu: Borrow Records

  Three tabs: Borrow, Item and Service. Each is a list of your past
  and current requests. You can page through the list and print it.

3.6 Department sharing

  Users in the same department share owned items, borrow records and
  requests. If your account has no department, you only see your own.

3.7 Settings

  Menu: Settings

  Change your name, phone number and password.

3.8 Get the mobile app

  Menu: Get App. Download and install the app, then see section 5.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ADMINISTRATOR GUIDE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 Dashboard

  The first page after login. It shows:
  - Summary cards for the whole inventory.
  - A campus inventory line chart and a campus summary.
  - Doughnut charts of item and request status.
  - Quick actions and lists of recent requests and recent items.

  The sidebar leads to every admin page.

4.2 Inventory

  Menu: Inventory

  Adding an item:
  1. Choose Add Item.
  2. Enter the name, category, department, location, cost, condition,
     model, serial number and quantity.
  3. Save. One record is created for each unit, and each unit gets its
     own QR code. New units start as Available.

  Other actions:
  - Edit an item or one unit.
  - View or print a unit's QR code to stick on the equipment.
  - Search and filter by category, status and owner.

  Items cannot be deleted. To remove old equipment from use, use
  Condemnation (section 4.5).

4.3 Campuses and departments

  Menu: Campuses

  - Campus cards show each campus and its items.
  - Choose Manage Departments to add or remove colleges, offices and
    campuses.
  - Built-in entries are protected and cannot be deleted. Entries you
    add yourself can be deleted.

4.4 Requests

  Menu: Requests

  This is where you decide on requests and see them through to
  delivery.

  1. Filter the list by type, status, urgency or campus. Switch
     between table view and tracker view.
  2. Open a request. Actions apply to the whole group of units that
     were submitted together.
  3. Choose a decision:

     APPROVE      The request moves on. If the user asked for an item
                  that is not in the catalog, a new inventory record
                  is created at this point.
     DISAPPROVE   You must type a reason. The reserved unit is released
                  and the user is told why.

  4. For an approved request, choose Mark Out for Delivery. The user
     receives an email and a notification.
  5. When the item has been handed over, choose Mark Delivered. The
     user can also confirm delivery by scanning the unit's QR code in
     the mobile app, which has the same effect.
  6. For a borrow request, delivery sets the unit to Borrowed and
     starts a borrow record. When the item comes back, set its actual
     return date. The record closes and the unit becomes Available
     again.
  7. Item and service requests are complete once delivered.

4.5 Condemnation

  Menu: Condemnation

  Use this page to retire equipment that can no longer be used.

  - Evaluate tab: units still in service.
  - Condemned tab: units you have condemned.
  - Disposed tab: units that have been disposed of.

  Steps:
  1. On the Evaluate tab, choose Condemn for a unit. You must enter a
     reason. The date and your name are saved.
  2. The unit moves to the Condemned tab.
  3. From there, choose Dispose when the equipment has been junked,
     sold or donated. The disposal date is saved.
  4. If the unit turns out to be usable, choose Restore. It becomes
     Available again.

4.6 Users

  Menu: Users

  - See every account with its role and department.
  - View a profile.
  - Deactivate an account so the person can no longer log in, or
    activate it again.
  - Assign or change a user's campus or college.

4.7 Analytics and Reports

  Menu: Analytics, Reports

  - Analytics shows charts of inventory and request activity.
  - Reports are printable summaries.

4.8 Settings

  Menu: Settings

  - Update your admin profile and password.
  - Terminology: switch the site wording between Borrow / Request and
    Transferrable / Procurement. The change applies to every page for
    every user.
  - Borrow catalog page size: how many items the borrow catalog shows
    on each page.
  - Borrow catalog tab: a read-only view of the catalog.

5. MOBILE APP
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The ManageMo mobile app is for confirming deliveries and looking up
items. Download it from the Get App page.

  - Pending deliveries: lists the items that are out for delivery to
    you.
  - Confirm delivery: scan the QR code on each unit. Scanning marks it
    delivered in the system.
  - Check item details: scan any unit's QR code to see its details.
    This is read-only.

6. STATUS REFERENCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Request status

  pending       Submitted and waiting for an administrator.
  approved      An administrator accepted it.
  disapproved   An administrator declined it. A reason is given.
  delivered     The item has reached the user.
  completed     The request is finished.

Delivery status

  out_for_delivery   Approved and on its way to the user.
  delivered          Handed over.

Item (unit) status

  available     Can be requested.
  requested     Held for a pending request.
  borrowed      Lent out.
  maintenance   Being repaired.
  damaged       Broken and waiting for a decision.
  condemned     Marked as no longer usable.
  disposed      Condemned and then junked, sold or donated.
  owned         Permanently given to a user through an Item request.

Borrow record status

  active        Item is out.
  returned      Item came back.
  overdue       Item was not returned by its due date.

7. TROUBLESHOOTING
----------------------------------------------------------------------

I cannot log in.
  Check the email and password. Use Forgot Password if needed. If the
  details are correct, ask an administrator whether your account is
  active.

I did not get the password reset email.
  Check your spam folder, then try again. Make sure you typed the
  same email you registered with.

An item I want is not on the list.
  Only available items can be requested. Check the Borrowed tab for
  its return date, or submit an Item request with the name of what
  you need.

I cannot find a menu or page.
  Users and administrators see different menus. If you expect an
  admin page, check with the system owner that your account has the
  admin role.

My request is still "Under Review".
  An administrator has not decided yet. Urgent requests should be set
  to a higher urgency when you submit them.

My item is out for delivery but I have not received it.
  Contact the administrator who handled your request. Quote the
  request number shown on your request card.

I scanned the wrong QR code in the app.
  Tell an administrator. They can correct the delivery status from the
  Requests page.
